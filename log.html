IT and Security Operations #1 for Agentic AI adoption

November 3, 2026

I’ve been exploring Agentic AI security.

I’d like to better understand who is adopting agentic systems and what business functions and tasks they are performing.

Agentic AI poses security risks. So in defining the set of companies that have the most urgency to solve Agentic AI security risks, I’d like to understand:

  1. What business functions/tasks are most common
  2. What Industries and verticals are adopting
  3. The risks/consequences of a security breach

What I learned: IT and Security Operations is the #1 for Agentic AI adoption.

What this means is kinda interesting: It means that Agentic AI security solutions are procured in large part to manage the risks of other security products.

Some interesting things to note:

Agentic AI definition: Given the sources for the research are in many cases surveys, there is inconsistency across survey and from the respondents themselves on definitions. People are confused about what an “agent” is versus “agentic AI”.

Because of this confusion, customer service & support ranks as #1 in the table below. But that is because respondents call their LLM chatbot an AGENT because it is performing the role of customer service AGENT - not because it is actually AGENTIC. The customer success agent is not necessarily agentic because it isn’t taking autonomous actions.

Some customer service systems might indeed be agentic - for instance writing to CRM systems, or interacting with other systems autonomously.

But. let’s assume that the #1 Agentic Business Task is incorrectly represented because of this confusion.

That means IT Operations and Security Operations are the #1 and #2 uses of agentic AI

Lesson:

If I were selling and marketing AI Security to the Enterprise, I would focus on technology companies and financial services.

Within those organizations, I’d focus on the IT Operations and Security Operations teams. This is very interesting. It means that Agentic AI security solutions are procured in large part to manage the risks of other security products.
