October 1, 2025

I love to learn - and want to catch up quickly on what I can do with my coding background and available AI tools
Step 1 - Build Local AI Development Environment

* For a local AI playground got a GMKtec mini PC - the EVO-X1. Features the AMD Ryzen Al 9 HX 370 as a CPU and the Radeon 890M GPU

* Installed Ubuntu 24.04 as dual boot - intention is to use Ubuntu operating system and keep my activities firewalled from other workstations.

* Installed Docker and Docker image released from AMD for Pytorch with ROCm support - required to leverage the GPU for faster processing
